## 1. Headline
- **A "share of headcount under 30" panel is feasible** for FY22–FY25 across 9 of the 10 firms, and for FY20–FY26 for about 6 of them.
  - The source is voluntary GRI annexes and ESG data books. BRSR has no age fields.
  - Gaps: Coforge has FY25–26 only. TCS is India-only, read off charts, FY20–25. Hexaware reports by calendar year.
- **"New hires under 30"** is feasible for FY21/22–FY25 at 8 firms.
  - Missing: TCS (nothing after FY17) and Coforge (no hires by age).
  - Holes: HCL FY23 and Wipro FY26.
- **The under-30 share of headcount fell from FY23 at 8 of 10 firms, and mid-tier growth did not come with a junior ladder.**
  - Persistent fell from 47.6% to 27.5% over FY23–26: its under-30 headcount fell from 10.9k to 7.6k (−31%) while total headcount rose 20%. The under-30 share of its hires fell from 63% to 37%.
  - Mphasis fell from 45.1% to 37.3% (FY25).
  - Hexaware (about 42–44%) and Coforge (36.6%, FY25–26) were flat.
- **Junior hiring at the large firms has partly recovered.**
  - The under-30 share of new hires rose from 60% to 70% to 73% at Infosys (FY24–26), and from 50% to 51% to 57% at LTIM.
  - The Infosys trainee proxy bottomed in FY24 at its lowest level since FY11 (about 1.2–1.9% of effort) and recovered to about 3–5% in FY26.
  - Median remuneration grew more slowly than wage hikes at Infosys in FY26 (+3.8% against an 11% average hike) and fell in level at Wipro (−0.6% FY25, −3.2% FY26). Both fit a renewed junior intake.
- **Best three metrics:**
  1. Under-30 share of new hires.
  2. Under-30 share of headcount.
  3. Infosys job levels plus the trainee proxy, as a high-frequency check.

  Median remuneration is a useful cross-check for Infosys, TCS and Wipro only.

**BRSR requirements (CONFIRMED from SEBI documents in `raw/sebi/`)**
- **When it applies.** SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD-2/P/CIR/2021/562 (10 May 2021), para 7: BRSR is mandatory for the top 1,000 listed companies by market capitalisation from FY2022-23, and voluntary for FY2021-22.
  - Source: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/inline-files/SEBI_Circular_10052021.pdf, p.2
- **What the updated format asks for** (Annexure II, BRSR Core circular, 12 Jul 2023: https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/commondocs/jul-2023/Annexure_II-Updated-BRSR_p.PDF):
  - Section A Q20: employees and workers by **gender only**.
  - Q22: turnover by **gender**.
  - Principle 5 Q3a: **median remuneration by gender**.
  - **No age bands, no new-hire counts, no grade split.**
- Age tables therefore come only from voluntary GRI 401-1 and 405-1 annexes. The GRI standard text itself was not fetched (404); the age bands were inferred from the firms' GRI-labelled tables.

## 3. What each metric shows about juniors and seniors

1. **Under-30 share of headcount (stock).**
   - A slow-moving summary of the pyramid.
   - It falls mechanically when a large intake cohort ages past 30 (the FY22 surge), when junior attrition falls, and with long-run pyramid ageing (Infosys was already falling about 2 pp a year over FY15–21).
   - It cannot separate "fewer juniors hired" from "juniors stayed and aged".
   - Scope differs: global vs India; Persistent, Coforge and Hexaware include contract staff.
2. **Under-30 share and count of new hires (flow).** The most direct measure of the mobility ladder.
   - Under-30 is not the same as fresher: laterals with 2–7 years' experience also count.
   - Mphasis's age rows don't sum to its totals.
   - Hexaware's CY24 hires were restated (30–50 and >50 swapped); the under-30 share is unaffected.
   - HCL's bands are 18–30 / 31–50 / 51+.
   - Wipro dropped hires by age in FY26.
3. **Turnover by age.** Separates "juniors pushed out" from "juniors not hired". Definitions break often (total vs voluntary, IT-only, last twelve months, counts vs rates), so compare within a firm only.
4. **Job level or grade.**
   - Infosys JL3-and-below is the only clean junior band: 46% of headcount in FY23, 30% in FY26 (158k → 99k). The FY23 break (Senior absorbed Top) does not affect Junior.
   - The junior or associate categories at TechM, LTIM, Persistent and Hexaware cover 78–90% of staff, which is too coarse.
   - TCS published a BRSR band table (junior about 55% of the banded headcount, FY22–23) and then dropped it.
5. **Fresher counts.** The most policy-relevant, but stated only ad hoc in calls and annual reports, with none for the mid-tier firms. Definitions vary: offers, joiners, or trained.
6. **Trainee proxy (Infosys).**
   - Trainee effort share = 1 − U_incl/U_excl, quarterly from FY11.
   - Calibrated against reported trainee counts for FY11–18: implied count = 0.73 × share × software professionals (corr 0.70, n=32).
   - About 8.4% in Q1–Q2 FY23; 1.2% in Q3FY24, the series low; 3.0–4.9% in FY25–26.
   - Not usable elsewhere: TechM's pair has been about 0 since FY23, LTI's incl. series stops at Q2FY23, and TCS stopped in FY16.
7. **Median remuneration (Companies Act s.197(12), Rule 5(1)).**
   - Test: compare growth in median pay with the stated average wage hike. Faster growth means the mix is shifting toward seniors; slower means toward juniors.
   - Caveats:
     - It is a standalone-entity figure (India plus overseas branches).
     - Hikes are given as ranges (TCS), are missing (Wipro), or include promotions (Infosys).
     - HCL reports a global PPP median.
     - Onsite/offshore mix and INR moves confound it.
   - FY24: median pay jumped at TCS (+10.8% against 5.5–8% hikes) and Wipro (+9.4%) while headcount fell, which fits fewer juniors.
   - FY25–26: it lagged hikes at Infosys, fell at Wipro and was flat at Persistent, which fits a renewed junior intake.
   - Hexaware CY25 (+8.45% against 3.9%) and HCL FY26 (+5.4% against 2.97%) point the other way.
8. **Employee cost per average headcount (TCS, HCL, Wipro, TechM).**
   - FY24 rose about 10 log points at all four despite muted hikes, consistent with a shift away from juniors.
   - Confounded by INR moves (onsite pay) and one-offs: Wipro FY22 (Capco acquisition), TCS FY26 (possible severance).
   - The weakest metric; illustrative only.
9. **Average age or experience.** Not reported after FY20 by any firm. Persistent reports average tenure for FY26 only.

**Definitional breaks to carry into any panel**
- Infosys: turnover basis changes in FY22; Senior includes Top from FY23; Stater restatement in FY22–23.
- TCS: age shares are India-only and read off charts (FY16 is global).
- LTIM: stitch LTI and Mindtree before FY23 (merger 14 Nov 2022); LTI has no FY22 report.
- TechM: FY18–20 hires are India-only.
- Wipro: excludes Capco and acquisitions.
- Coforge: India-only, including contract staff; the FY25 report's columns look swapped and FY26 restates FY25.
- Persistent: the FY26 fact sheet's turnover column headings are shifted by a year.
- Hexaware: calendar years; the 2025 sustainability report restates turnover to voluntary IT-only (CY22: 15,316 → 3,748).
