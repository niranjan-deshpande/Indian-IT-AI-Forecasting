# TechM / LTIMindtree workforce extract: notes

**Sources.**
- **TechM:** the Integrated Annual Report (IAR) ESG section has global GRI tables for FY21-FY26 (pp. ~60-135). These cover headcount by age x gender x grade, new hires by age, and voluntary IT attrition % by age, gender and grade. FY18-20 come from the older Integrated Reports and are mostly India only.
- **LTIMindtree/LTM, Mindtree, LTI:** Sustainability Report scorecards (ltm.com/.../uploads/report/) give headcount, new hires, attrition counts and turnover % by age and gender.

**Grade data.**
- TechM: bands U1-E3 for FY18-21 and FY23-24.
- Mindtree: grades C-C12 for FY15-22.
- LTI: categories including "Trainee" for FY18-21.

**Not disclosed.**
- TechM attrition counts by age.
- The TechM FY19 age split.
- LTI FY22 (no sustainability report).
- Median/average employee age, tenure or experience, for any firm.
- LTM headcount by level.

**Freshers** come mostly from earnings-call transcripts. Gaps: TechM FY16-20 and FY23; LTI FY16-17 and FY19-21; Mindtree FY16-18 and FY20; LTIM FY23-24 full-year.

**Caveats.**
- TechM FY18-20 hires are India only.
- The TechM FY19 IR contains two conflicting age tables.
- Mindtree's FY21 hires were restated from 5,939 to 10,298.
- TechM's FY25 attrition by age looks anomalous.
- Attrition is defined differently: LTIM counts all exits over end-of-period headcount, while TechM reports voluntary IT-only LTM rates.
- The merger closed on 14 Nov 2022, so FY23 is the first combined year.
