# Literature search: 2_industry_sellside (2026-10-15)

Nobody in this lane has published a full health check like ours. Industry bodies (Nasscom, Zinnov) publish sector totals, brokers write about revenue and AI deflation, and staffing and job-board firms track fresher demand, but none combines firm results, BoP exports, GCCs, PLFS and junior pay into one verdict. The closest single source is Nasscom's Strategic Review 2026, which itself says revenue growth (6.1%) is outpacing headcount growth (2.3%). But Nasscom and Goldman both say the wider sector is still a net hirer because of GCCs, so "stopped adding jobs" holds for the listed IT services firms and is contested for the sector as a whole.

**Most relevant items, ranked**

1. **Nasscom Strategic Review 2026**, Feb 24, 2026 (press coverage; the Nasscom PDF would not load). https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/corporate/story/it-industry-likely-to-hire-135000-employees-in-fy26-projects-nasscom-517760-2026-02-24 and https://www.etvbharat.com/en/business/india-fy26-tech-revenues-hit-usd-315-billion-enn26022404879
   - **Data:** FY26 revenue of $315B (+6.1%), exports of about $246B, direct employees 5.95M with about 135k net added (FY25: about 133k), AI revenue $10–12B. FY25 revenue was revised up to $297B.
   - **Claim:** a "non-linearity" between revenue and jobs. Most hiring growth comes from GCCs, and campus hiring is "down significantly."
   - **Overlap:** it measures the same gap we do. **Contradicts:** it says the sector is still adding jobs. We should either limit the claim to IT services firms or make the GCC offset explicit. The headcount series is data we may lack.

2. **Kotak Institutional Equities**, Apr 29, 2026 (https://news.webindia123.com/news/Articles/Business/20260429/4445043.html) and Aug 7, 2026 (https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/ai-led-deflation-weak-demand-keep-it-sector-growth-358.htm).
   - **Data:** Q4 results, company guidance, Gartner forecasts (IT services growth 3.1% in 2025, 4.2% forecast for 2026).
   - **Claim:** growth stays range-bound near 3%, with a 3.5% AI-deflation base case. HCLTech's own estimate is 3–5%. Tier-1 guidance clusters at 1–4%. Mid-tier firms are better placed. Kotak calls the deflation cyclical, not permanent.
   - **Overlap:** prices and demand. It says nothing on jobs.

3. **JPMorgan sector note**, Jun 30, 2026. https://www.peoplematters.in/news/ai-and-emerging-tech/as-ai-slows-it-growth-jp-morgan-expects-tcs-and-infosys-to-outperform-wipro-hcl-tech-50585
   - **Claim:** the industry has been "stuck at 2-3%" growth for three years. AI deflation is "only in Year 2." It sees 3–4% medium-term growth and downgraded HCLTech and Wipro.
   - **Overlap:** backs the "output still growing, slowly" half of our claim. No labour data.

4. **ICICI Securities, "Indian IT Sector Analysis 2026"**, Mar 30, 2026. https://www.icicidirect.com/research/equity/finace/indian-it-sector-analysis-2026-valuation-growth-ai-risks-and-top-picks
   - **Claim:** traditional services deflate 2–3% a year, peaking FY26–28. About 30% of the industry is at risk, a $40B hit to India in the base case and $80–85B in the worst case. It puts the new AI opportunity at $300–400B by 2030.
   - **Overlap:** the most explicit sizing of AI deflation we have; worth citing for our demand-response parameter. No headcount data.

5. **Nasscom–Zinnov, India GCC Landscape 2026**, about May 2026. https://zinnov.com/centers-of-excellence/zinnov-nasscom-india-gcc-landscape-2026-report/
   - **Data:** 2,117 GCCs, 3,728 units, 2.36M staff, $98.4B revenue, count up 32% since FY21. Press coverage says 30% of GCC hires are early-career.
   - **Overlap:** our GCC section. These are the counts we should use.

6. **Goldman Sachs (economist Santanu Sengupta)**, Aug 14 and Oct 1, 2026. https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/goldman-sachs-says-ai-unlikely-to-cause-widespread-job-losses-in-india-services-sector-faces-risk and https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/goldman-sachs-pushes-back-on-short-ai-india-narrative-says-report-42-stocks-in-focus-559148-2026-10-01
   - **Claim:** hiring by GCCs and smaller tech firms is offsetting job losses in traditional software services, so widespread job loss is unlikely. A sustained fall in junior openings is the risk to watch.
   - **Contradicts** the jobs half of our claim, and supports the entry-level half. One secondary site credits Goldman with "top 6 shed ~64k, ecosystem added ~700k." I could not verify that, so don't cite it.

7. **Fresher and entry-level demand** (all strongly support our entry-level claim):
   - **TeamLease EdTech Career Outlook H2 2026**, Aug 2026, 1,097 employers. IT fresher hiring intent fell from 81% to 76% while overall intent rose to 75%. https://www.peoplematters.in/amp/news/workforce-planning/fresher-hiring-intent-rises-to-75percent-but-it-demand-loses-ground-report-51352
   - **foundit Insights Tracker**, Jul 2026. IT's share of fresher hiring fell from 32% to 24%, and overall fresher hiring fell 10% year on year. https://www.thehansindia.com/hans/young-hans/employers-seek-more-specialised-skills-from-freshers-survey-1122312
   - **Xpheno, via Outlook Business**, Jun 1, 2026. Entry-level tech openings (0–2 years' experience) were 10k, down 44% year on year. Active tech openings were 93k in June, the lowest in 28 months. Infosys staff aged 30 and under fell to 50.7%, the lowest in 15 years. https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/india-incs-fresher-hiring-sees-steep-drop-amid-ai-adoption

8. **IANS headcount tally**, Apr 25, 2026. The top five firms' combined headcount fell by 7,389 in FY26, against a gain of 12,718 in FY25. https://ianslive.in/top-it-firms-hiring-turns-negative-as-headcount-falls-over-7000-in-fy26--20260425104000

9. **BNP Paribas**, early Oct 2026 (secondary coverage). A qualitative "AI-led deflation" note: the link between adding engineers and growing revenue is weakening, and large caps are more exposed. No numbers. https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/brokerage-reports/BNP-Paribas-AI-Led-Deflation-Pressures-Indian-IT-Sector/6abf2f9d5aacb956d08943cc

**Counter-signals to address**
- **TCS is hiring again:** +4,258 in Q2FY27, its third straight quarter of net additions, with 25k fresher offers for FY27. https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/tcs-adds-4258-employees-in-q2-marks-third-quarter-of-headcount-addition-560460-2026-10-08
- **Naukri JobSpeak, Feb 2026:** an IT hiring "recovery" and fresher hiring up 17%. That reversed by June, when overall IT hiring was down 3%.
- **Kotak and ICICI frame the deflation as a transition.** Both read it as cyclical, not structural.
