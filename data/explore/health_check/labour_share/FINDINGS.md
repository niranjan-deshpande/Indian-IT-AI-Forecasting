## FINDINGS.md

### 1. Bottom line
- **The share is not falling on any accounts-based measure.** It sits at about 0.65–0.72 at company level, rose FY22→FY24 (a margin squeeze), then went flat.
- **The broader measures are rising:**
  - KLEMS Business services: 0.426 (FY16) → 0.502 (FY23).
  - NAS group, private corporations: 0.548 (FY16) → 0.645 (FY24) on the 2011-12 base, and 0.744 → 0.759 (FY23–25) on the 2022-23 base.
- **The Substack's fall is a survey artefact,** not a change in the economy.
- **Real junior IT pay** rose to CY2024, then fell 12% in CY2025.

### 2. Definitions
| Measure | Labour share = | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Firm (top-6, mid-3) | employee benefit expense ÷ (employee cost + EBITDA) | Consolidated, global. EBITDA excludes other income; subcontracting counts as a purchased input. |
| RBI listed IT | staff cost ÷ (staff cost + operating profit) | Standalone accounts of 192–274 listed IT firms; not a fixed panel |
| NAS group, private corporations | compensation of employees ÷ GVA (Statement 7.1B, item 9) | IT is about 55–60% of this group |
| NAS group minus dwellings | compensation of employees ÷ (group GVA − dwellings GVA) | All sectors in the group |
| KLEMS Business services | labour income (includes imputed self-employed income) ÷ GVA | IT plus legal, consulting and R&D |
| ASUSE information & communication | pay to hired workers ÷ GVA | Unincorporated firms only; about ₹5k crore of GVA |
| Substack method | PLFS earnings × employment × 12 ÷ NAS IT GVA | Survey take-home pay |

### 3. What can be separated for IT
- **NAS 8.13, item 3** gives IT GVA (current prices, before the FISIM bank-services adjustment), in ₹ lakh crore.
  - 2011-12 base: 6.61 (FY18), 8.70 (FY20), 11.75 (FY22), 14.78 (FY23), 16.49 (FY24).
  - 2022-23 base: 15.86, 17.80, 19.97 (FY23–25).
- **Pay to employees and operating surplus** (NAS 7.1, 7.1A–C) exist only for the broad group. Do not splice the two bases.
- **ASSSE pilot:** no industry estimates. **ASUSE:** information & communication only.
- **KLEMS:** the July 2024 release is the latest, with data to FY23.
- **RBI study of public limited companies (Statement 12):** now no finer than information & communication, which includes telecom.

### 5. Match with the Substack chart
- **Value added:** matches NAS 8.13 exactly (6.6 → 16.5 on the old base; 15.9 / 17.8 / 20.0 on the new).
- **Wage bill:** rebuilt from repo PLFS aggregates only, not unit data. It comes to ₹0.84 / 3.50 / 3.33 lakh crore against the Substack's about 0.9 / 3.8 / 3.7. Growth over 2017-18 to 2023-24 is 4.2× against their 4.0×.
- **The level is far too low.** The top-6 firms' employee cost alone (₹3.9 lakh crore in FY24) exceeds the PLFS wage bill for the whole sector (₹3.5 lakh crore). PLFS excludes employer provident fund, under-captures high earners and misses onsite pay.
- **The 2017–24 rise** reflects PLFS IT employment growing 2.5×, against about 1.4× for Nasscom.
- **The 2024-25 fall** comes from the redesigned CY2025 survey paired with FY25 value added. RBI's listed-IT share over the same years is .687 → .686.

### 6. Caveats
- **Firm figures are global:** they mix onsite and offshore staff. HCLTech includes HCLSoftware; TechM's FY24 spike reflects its restructuring.
- **Lease accounting:** a lease-accounting change in FY20 (Ind AS 116) lowers the share by about 1–2 points from FY20.
- **Firm data source:** employee costs come from Tickertape and Screener. Cross-checks: repo panel median difference 0% (max 1.8%), and 11 primary filings within 1%.
- **RBI sample:** firm count changes by year (211 → 274 over FY24–26), so levels are not a common sample.
- **NAS group measures** may rise partly because IT's weight within the group is growing. The new base lifts the group's share by about 9 points.
- **KLEMS** uses a fixed imputation for self-employed income.
- **CPI:** the base-2024 series was not available from the MoSPI API, so FY26 was not deflated. That does not affect CY2025.
- **CY2025 PLFS** is a redesigned survey with a 2.5× larger sample.
