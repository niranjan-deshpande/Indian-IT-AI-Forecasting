# HCLTech / Wipro workforce extract: notes

Built by `build_extract_hcl_wipro.py`; pages are physical PDF pages.

**HCLTech**
- Age tables:
  - SR FY26 annex pp84–86: headcount, hires and attrition by age, FY24–26. Hire/attrition bands are 18-30 / 31-50 / 51+.
  - SR 2022-23 p37 and SR 2024 p50: headcount by age × gender, FY20–24.
  - SR 2022 p34: hires by age and by management level, FY21–22.
- Fresher counts: FY22 from the Q4FY22 investor release; FY23–26 from the annual reports.
- Not disclosed:
  - hires by age for FY23; attrition by age before FY24
  - annual fresher counts for FY16–21
  - headcount by band (11 bands exist but are not tabulated), average/median age, tenure
- Caveats:
  - Gen Z share is the only age proxy in the annual reports.
  - FY16 was a 9-month year (fiscal-year change).

**Wipro**
- ESG Dashboards FY21–26: headcount by age FY19–26; hires and voluntary attrition by age FY19–25.
- The FY26 dashboard dropped the hires/attrition-by-age tables. The FY26 sustainability report is not yet online.
- Scope: permanent employees, excluding Capco and acquisitions. The FY26 BRSR headcount includes Capco.
- Restatements: BRSR turnover FY22 (22.9 → 23.71) and FY23 (20.4 → 19.21).
- Fresher counts come only from transcripts and annual reports: FY23 total, plus quarterly FY20 and FY22–23 figures.
- The news-reported "7,500 freshers FY26" is absent from the Wipro-hosted transcript, so it was not recorded.
- Average age is available for FY16 only.
- No grade, tenure or experience data.
