# Literature search: 3_journalism_substack (2026-10-15)

**Bottom line:** I found one close match. A Substack called "Future of India" ran a PLFS-based piece on 2 October 2026 that makes nearly our exact claim: output keeps growing, jobs and pay don't. But it has no firm-level, GCC, mid-tier or price data. Nobody I found combines firm results, RBI exports, GCCs, PLFS, fresher hiring and prices in one piece. The topic itself is very crowded, though. Most coverage is short news or opinion with few charts, set off by the February and May–June 2026 IT stock sell-offs. The main data-led counter-argument (Baldwin, ING, Noah Smith) is that exports are booming.

**Most relevant items, ranked**

1. **"IT Sector Helped Build the Indian Middle Class. For Its Workers, It Is Starting to Sputter"**, Future of India Substack (no named author; first published on YouthPOWER), 2 Oct 2026. https://foifaction.substack.com/p/it-sector-helped-build-the-indian
   - **Data:** PLFS (including the 2025 round), RBI software-export survey, NASSCOM, MoSPI value added. Six charts.
   - **Claim:** IT employment, salaried-graduate IT jobs, real earnings and labour's share of value added all peaked in 2022-23 or 2023-24. The share of salaried graduates aged 21–25 working in IT fell from 20.0% to 15.8%. Developer and analyst jobs fell about 14%.
   - **Overlap:** This is very high, and it uses PLFS microdata as we do. Worth checking whether it is connected to anyone on our side.
   - **What it adds:** a labour-share-of-value-added chart and a real median earnings chart, which we may lack.

2. **"India's $300B AI Shock"**, Aman Kai Sidhant (a VC investor), 18 Jun 2026. https://india300.ai/
   - **Data:** NASSCOM, RBI, company filings, PLFS, BLS. Many charts, including TCS revenue against headcount, RBI BPO sub-categories and fresher projections.
   - **Claim:** AI cuts jobs before it cuts revenue. It then builds scenarios for tax, the current account and consumption.
   - **Difference:** a bearish argument built on scenarios, not a health check.

3. **Richard Baldwin, "The Jobs AI Was Supposed to Eliminate Are Booming"**, 30–31 Jul 2026. https://rbaldwin.substack.com/p/the-jobs-ai-was-supposed-to-eliminate
   - **Data:** WTO "other business services" exports for nine economies, plus RBI.
   - **Claim:** India's exports are about 27% above their 2015–22 trend.
   - **Related:** Noah Smith amplified it ("AI keeps stubbornly refusing to kill Indian back-office outsourcing", https://www.noahpinion.blog/p/roundup-88-is-it-time-to-panic-yet, 18 Sep). Marcus Nunes rebutted it on 2 Aug, arguing export value is not employment (https://marcusnunes.substack.com/p/a-critical-look-at-the-jobs-ai-was).
   - **How it bears on us:** it supports our "output growing" half. We should cite the debate.

4. **ING, Deepali Bhargava, 16 Sep 2026.** The original note wasn't found; I verified it through Business Today on 18 Sep: https://www.businesstoday.in/jobs/story/reshaping-not-shrinking-how-indias-205-billion-tech-engine-defies-job-threats-in-ai-era-556430-2026-09-18 (Bloomberg's 17 Sep piece was blocked).
   - **Claim:** India's outsourcing is "reshaping, not shrinking". Software exports are 5.2% of GDP, up from 3.3% before the pandemic. India's digitally delivered exports grew 45% since 2022, against 32% worldwide.
   - **How it bears on us:** this contradicts the alarm framing. It does not contradict the jobs data.

   - **Claim:** India's knowledge work is gaining while the outsourcing firms lose. It has no PLFS or fresher data.

**Evidence that complicates our working claim**
- **TCS is hiring again.** It added 9,279 staff in Q1 FY27 and 4,258 in Q2, its third straight quarterly net gain, reported 8 Oct: https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/tcs-adds-4258-employees-in-q2-marks-third-quarter-of-headcount-addition-560460-2026-10-08. It is still about 15k below a year earlier.
- **Fresher hiring hasn't stopped everywhere.** Infosys is holding at about 20k freshers a year, and Cognizant plans 24–25k in 2026.
- **The industry body says jobs grew.** NASSCOM estimates about +135k (+2.3%) for FY26.
- **Suggested wording:** "Stopped adding jobs" should be stated as top-five net hiring that was flat or negative over FY26 and only partly recovered in FY27.

**Datasets or charts we may lack**

- Unearthinsight's revenue-per-employee series for FY19–26.
- Labour share of IT value added, as in item 1.
- Baldwin's cross-country WTO index against the pre-AI trend.
- RBI BPO sub-category trends, as in item 2.
- The Economic Survey 2025-26 estimate that AI-exposed services exports rose about 39.5% without proportionate job gains. I saw this only in secondary coverage.
